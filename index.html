    Before you use this

    • Prayer times shift daily. The five prayer blocks below are placed where they fall in early September — check a prayer times app for your area and nudge the block times as the year moves on, especially Maghrib and Isha, which get earlier through autumn.
    • Fajr on weekends: set an alarm for the actual Fajr time even on Saturday/Sunday, pray, then go back to sleep if you want the lie-in — don't let the weekend wake-up time replace it.
    • Jummah on Friday: your Period 4 Maths (12:05–13:05) may clash with Friday prayer depending on your mosque's timing. Worth checking with your college's pastoral/faith team this week about how they handle this — most colleges have a process.
    • Your college day already has big free periods — Wednesday from 12:30 and Tuesday 11:05–14:20 especially. Use them for topic review and past-paper questions so your evenings stay lighter and you can actually hit that 21:30 bedtime.
    • Maths and Chemistry get the most slots since they're your weakest — Economics gets protected time to push the C higher, since it's your strongest evidence for a Level 6 application.
